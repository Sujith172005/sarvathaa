Bake to Business Program

Bake to Business Program is designed to help individuals transform their interest and skills in baking into a practical business opportunity. The program combines hands-on baking practice with essential knowledge required to plan, start, and manage a baking business successfully.

Participants can develop skills in cakes, breads, cookies, pastries, desserts, decoration, and other baked products, while also learning recipe standardization, product development, costing, pricing, portion control, packaging, and quality management.

The program also introduces important areas such as food safety, hygiene, production planning, inventory management, wastage control, and efficient use of equipment and raw materials.

Beyond baking skills, participants receive guidance on business planning, branding, marketing, customer handling, sales, and daily operations. They can understand how to select suitable products, calculate profitability, maintain consistent quality, and prepare their business for commercial production.

The Bake to Business Program provides a step-by-step pathway from learning and practising baking to developing products, planning a business, and building a sustainable baking venture. It is suitable for beginners, home bakers, aspiring entrepreneurs, and existing bakers looking to expand their business.
